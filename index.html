THE PROJECT

A more informed starting point.

Personal Keyword Research is an independently developed research tool intended for the project owner's own use. It helps compare product-related search terms and identify broad demand patterns before making business decisions.

01

Historical demand

Request approximate monthly search volumes and historical averages through the Google Ads API.

02

Seasonality

Review monthly patterns to distinguish persistent demand from seasonal peaks.

03

Local analysis

Export authorised research results for private comparison and analysis in local spreadsheets or scripts.

DATA USE & TRANSPARENCY

Built for personal research.

The planned integration requests Google Ads keyword-planning metrics for an account the operator is authorised to access. The public website does not provide sign-in, collect Google credentials, or offer a keyword query service to visitors. Actual usage and safeguards are described in our Privacy Policy.
